# Relational EX — Scope

## Product thesis

Relational EX is an experimental instrument for exploring relationships between **pitch, rhythm, spectrum, tuning and motion**.

Microtonality is not a decorative mode. It is one consequence of a larger relational system.

## Core systems

1. **Frequency Field** — editable representation of tuning and pitch relationships.
2. **Relational Engine** — a compact coupling model linking pitch, time, FM, resonance and tuning.
3. **FM + Inharmonic Resonator** — the sound engine.
4. **Relational Sequencer** — events carrying musical and spectral properties, with Field/Grid/Radial views.
5. **Tuning Interoperability** — TET, Scala, KBM, MIDI Tuning Standard and MTS-ESP.
6. **Motion Layer** — LFO, deterministic chaos and tuning drift.
7. **Exploratory Presets** — presets designed to teach relationships, not genre labels.

## Explicitly out of scope

Relational EX is not intended to become:

- a general subtractive synthesizer;
- a wavetable workstation;
- a sampler;
- a granular engine;
- a drum sampler;
- a large multi-effect suite;
- a generic preset collection;
- a clone of Wilsonic, Buchla instruments, Autechre or Aphex Twin equipment/software.

External references are conceptual and technical influences, not specifications to imitate.

## Design rule

Prefer **deeper relationships over more modules**. A new feature should strengthen the relationship between at least two existing domains or solve a real workflow problem.

## Completion criteria

A feature is considered complete only when:

- realtime behavior is safe;
- the Standalone and VST3 paths are considered;
- tooltips/manual documentation exist in English;
- state/preset implications are documented;
- tests or reproducible checks exist;
- the decision is recorded when it changes the architecture.
