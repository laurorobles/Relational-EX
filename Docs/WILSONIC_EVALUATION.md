# Wilsonic Evaluation

## What we should implement

- Wilson/Erv-style scale exploration as a tuning-lab concept.
- Geometric visualization of tuning relationships.
- Searchable tuning families and saved experiments.
- MTS-ESP interoperability.
- Import/export of .scl.

## What we should NOT do in v1

- Make RELATIONAL dependent on a Wilsonic install.
- Embed a second complete Wilsonic application inside the plugin.
- Copy Wilsonic's UI wholesale.

## Why

Wilsonic already demonstrates that interactive scale design, automation, standalone operation and MTS-ESP can coexist in JUCE. Our differentiator should be the integration of tuning with synthesis and the relational sequencer rather than being another Wilsonic controller.
