# Relational EX — Research Notes

## A. Why microtonality matters here

Microtonality is not being added primarily to produce exotic scales. In Relational EX, a tuning system changes the numerical relationships that feed the oscillator and resonator. This means tuning can alter both perceived pitch and spectral behavior.

## B. Ratios as a common language

The same mathematical concept—ratio—can describe:

- pitch intervals;
- FM carrier/modulator relationships;
- partial spacing;
- resonator modes;
- polymetric cycle lengths.

This motivates a relational rather than module-isolated architecture.

## C. Beating

Closely spaced frequencies create amplitude fluctuations at the difference frequency. This offers a simple route to movement without a conventional LFO. Future versions should expose controlled cluster generation and beating as a first-class sound-design concept.

## D. Inharmonicity

Inharmonic spectra are useful because they create strong object-like identities without requiring physical modelling of one specific instrument. A six-mode resonator is therefore treated as a controllable spectral abstraction.

## E. Sequencing

Traditional step sequencers encode events primarily as time slots. Relational EX treats an event as a bundle of values. This enables independent cycle lengths, probability, tuning offsets, spectral changes and microtiming.

## F. Wilsonic lesson

Interactive tuning visualization is valuable because a numerical list of cents is cognitively poor. Geometric views make interval relationships visible. Relational EX therefore treats the tuning map as an editable instrument surface.

## G. Autechre lesson

The conceptual reference is the use of custom computational systems as compositional instruments. The design goal is not to imitate a track or sound palette, but to let the musician construct a system whose behavior is richer than a conventional step sequencer.

## H. RDJ lesson

Multiple tuning systems can be treated as a creative resource. Tuning should therefore be easy to change, compare, morph and save rather than hidden behind a technical preference panel.
